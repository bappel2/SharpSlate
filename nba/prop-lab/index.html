NBA Prop Lab

About the NBA Prop Lab

The Prop Lab lists the NBA player props the sportsbooks have posted, in the main markets we track. For each prop it shows how often the player has finished over the line in recent games and the best price on the board with the book posting it. Where enough books price both sides, it also shows the fair odds once the book's margin is taken out.

How these numbers work, linked at the bottom of every page, explains how the fair odds and hit rates are worked out for each sport. Hit rates come from the player's own game log, so the trend behind each number is one tap away. It is research, never picks.

Where can I find NBA player props today?

Right here, free and with no account. The Prop Lab lists the player props the sportsbooks post for the night's games, with the best price on the board, the book posting it and the player's hit rate. The Prop Finder screens the same props by hit rate, best price, team, game and opponent defense, with the fair odds beside each row. Each team playing that night also has a page of its own props.

When do NBA props come out?

Books post NBA player props the night before or the morning of a game, and move them as injury news lands. For the games on DraftKings' main slate we pull them around 12:30 PM ET (or two hours before the first game on an early slate) and again about 40 minutes before the slate's first tip. Other games that night can carry lines from snapshots of the books taken through the day. The page updates within about half an hour, and Lines as of says when each game's prices were taken.

Which NBA props are covered?

Player props in points, rebounds, assists, threes made, points + rebounds + assists, points + rebounds, points + assists, rebounds + assists, steals, blocks and turnovers.

How is an NBA hit rate worked out?

It is the share of games in which the player went over the posted line: the last 10 games by default, or the last 5 or 20, this season, last season or every game against tonight's opponent, at home or away. The games come from ESPN's NBA box scores. Early in a season a hit rate rests on only a few games, so read it with the game log under it.

What are fair odds on a player prop?

The price with the sportsbook's margin taken out, so you can see what the market itself thinks of each side. It needs at least two books pricing both sides of the prop. How these numbers work, linked at the bottom of the page, shows the math.

Does SharpSlate give NBA picks?

No. SharpSlate is research: hit rates, fair odds and the best price, so you can check a line yourself. It never tells you what to bet.
