Privacy

How SharpSlate handles information about you. Effective October 16, 2026.

The short version

SharpSlate has no accounts, sign ups or forms, and it sets no cookies of its own. It never asks for your name, email or payment details, and it does not sell information about you or use it for advertising.

Page view counts

SharpSlate uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages and referring sites people use. It sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. Cloudflare processes these counts under its own privacy policy.

Services your browser contacts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves every page and data file. Fonts load from Google Fonts, team logos and player photos load from ESPN and the NFL, and the page view counter loads from Cloudflare. Your browser requests these files directly, so each of these services receives your IP address and basic browser details, as with any website. Each has its own privacy policy.

Saved on your device

If you upload your own projections to the DFS optimizer, they are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you come back, for up to 21 days. They never leave your device. The optimizer's Clear button or your browser's site data settings remove them.

If you email us

If you write to info@sharpslatesports.com, your email address and message are used only to reply.

Other sites

SharpSlate Leagues (leagues.sharpslatesports.com) is a separate site with its own sign in. This page covers sharpslatesports.com only.

Children

SharpSlate is meant for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.
