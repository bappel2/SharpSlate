About the College Football Prop Lab

The Prop Lab lists the college football player props the sportsbooks have posted for Sat Oct 31 · 47 games, in the markets we track: passing yards, passing TDs, interceptions, rushing yards, receptions, receiving yards and anytime TD. For each prop it shows how often the player has finished over the line in recent games and the best price on the board with the book posting it. Where enough books price both sides, it also shows the fair odds once the book's margin is taken out; anytime touchdown props show the books' average price instead.

How these numbers work, linked at the bottom of every page, explains how the fair odds and hit rates are worked out for each sport. Hit rates come from the player's own game log, so the trend behind each number is one tap away. It is research, never picks.

Where can I find college football player props today?

Right here, free and with no account. The Prop Lab lists the player props the sportsbooks post for every college football game that has them, from Thursday night through Saturday, with the best price on the board, the book posting it and the player's hit rate. The Prop Finder screens the same props by hit rate, best price, team, game and opponent defense, with the fair odds beside each row.

When do college football props come out?

Most books post Saturday's player props late in the week. We pull them Thursday and Friday at 6 PM ET, and Saturday at 9 AM and 2 PM ET, and the page updates within about half an hour of each pull. Once DraftKings has posted Saturday's slate, the waiting page says when the next pull lands.

Which college football props are covered?

Player props in passing yards, passing TDs, interceptions, rushing yards, receptions, receiving yards and anytime TD.

How is a college football hit rate worked out?

It is the share of games in which the player went over the posted line: the last 10 games by default, or the last 5 or 20, this season, last season or every game against this week's opponent, at home or away. The games come from ESPN's college box scores. Early in a season a hit rate rests on only a few games, so read it with the game log under it.

What are fair odds on a player prop?

The price with the sportsbook's margin taken out, so you can see what the market itself thinks of each side. It needs at least two books pricing both sides of the prop; anytime touchdown props show the books' average price instead. How these numbers work, linked at the bottom of the page, shows the math.

Does SharpSlate give college football picks?

No. SharpSlate is research: hit rates, fair odds and the best price, so you can check a line yourself. It never tells you what to bet.
