About College Football Research

Volume, passing, team pace and the defense table, built on ESPN's college box scores.

What each table shows

  • Volume: Volume is the part of a box score that carries forward. Touches are carries plus catches; shares are measured against the player's own team in the games he played.
  • Passing: Quarterbacks by volume and efficiency. College stats count sacks as runs, so rushing yards here are net of sacks.
  • Team pace: How much each offense runs plays, and how often it throws. Plays are pass attempts plus carries; sacks count as carries in college stats, so a sack reads as a run.
  • Defense vs position: What each defense has allowed to each position, ranked against the rest of the league.

SharpSlate is research, not picks. Projections, probabilities and fair odds only.
