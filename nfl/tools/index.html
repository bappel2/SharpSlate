NFL Betting Calculators

Free betting calculators

Five calculators that run on your own inputs, in your browser, for any bet: NFL, college football, NBA or MLB sides, totals, parlays and player props. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Informational, not advice.

They pair with the NFL Prop Lab: copy a prop's over and under prices into the no vig calculator to see its fair odds, or put a few props into the parlay calculator to see what they pay together.

On this page

  • Odds converter: American odds to decimal odds and implied probability, and back again.
  • No vig calculator (fair odds calculator): take the sportsbook's margin out of a two way market, a spread, a total or a player prop, to see each side's fair price and the book's hold.
  • Parlay calculator: the combined odds and payout of a parlay from each leg's American odds and your stake. The full parlay calculator page adds pushes, voided legs and each leg's fair odds.
  • Kelly calculator: a stake size from your win chance, the offered odds and your bankroll.
  • Expected value calculator: what a bet is worth from your fair chance, the posted odds and your stake.
