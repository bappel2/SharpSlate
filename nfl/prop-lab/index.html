About the NFL Prop Lab

The Prop Lab lists the NFL player props the sportsbooks have posted for Week 7 · Sun Oct 25 to Mon Oct 26 · 13 games, in the markets we track: passing yards, passing TDs, rushing yards, receptions, receiving yards, rushing TDs, receiving TDs and anytime TD. For each prop it shows how often the player has finished over the line in recent games and the best price on the board with the book posting it. Where enough books price both sides, it also shows the fair odds once the book's margin is taken out; anytime touchdown props show the books' average price instead.

How these numbers work, linked at the bottom of every page, explains how the fair odds and hit rates are worked out for each sport. Hit rates come from the player's own game log, so the trend behind each number is one tap away. It is research, never picks.

Where can I find NFL player props today?

Right here, free and with no account. The Prop Lab lists the player props the sportsbooks post for every game of the NFL week, Thursday night through Monday night, with the best price on the board, the book posting it and the player's hit rate. The Prop Finder screens the same props by hit rate, best price, team, game and opponent defense, with the fair odds beside each row.

When are NFL player props updated?

Books post player props through the week and move them up to kickoff. We pull them Sunday at 8:15, 9:45 and 11:45 AM and 1:15, 3:10 and 7:05 PM ET, Monday and Thursday at 9:45 AM and 7 PM, Tuesday at 9:45 AM, and Wednesday, Friday and Saturday at 9:45 AM and 5:45 PM, and the page updates within about half an hour of each pull. Lines as of, on the Prop Lab, says when the prices on screen were pulled.

Which NFL props are covered?

Player props in passing yards, passing TDs, rushing yards, receptions, receiving yards and anytime TD, plus rushing and receiving TDs when a book posts them.

How is an NFL hit rate worked out?

It is the share of games in which the player went over the posted line: the last 10 games by default, or the last 5 or 20, this season, last season or every game against this week's opponent, at home or away. The games come from nflverse's weekly player stats. Early in a season a hit rate rests on only a few games, so read it with the game log under it.

What are fair odds on a player prop?

The price with the sportsbook's margin taken out, so you can see what the market itself thinks of each side. It needs at least two books pricing both sides of the prop; anytime touchdown props show the books' average price instead. How these numbers work, linked at the bottom of the page, shows the math.

Does SharpSlate give NFL picks?

No. SharpSlate is research: hit rates, fair odds and the best price, so you can check a line yourself. It never tells you what to bet.
